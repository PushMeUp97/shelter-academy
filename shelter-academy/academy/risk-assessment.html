Domain 2: Information Risk Assessment Methodologies

Systematically identifying, evaluating, and prioritizing risk metrics to minimize unexpected asset disruption.

Identification of Vulnerabilities & Threats

Risk assessment begins with deep environmental visibility. Vulnerabilities are inherent weaknesses within an infrastructure, system, or process, while threats represent any circumstances with the potential to exploit those specific weaknesses.

Reconnaissance & Discovery Operations

Security teams conduct continuous technical identification activities, using discovery frameworks and network mapping processes to audit live endpoints, running software services, and exposed assets across network environments.

Qualitative vs. Quantitative Analysis

To establish clear corporate priorities, organizations measure risks using two foundational models:

Methodology Focus Metrics Pros / Benefits
Qualitative Analysis Subjective grading scales (e.g., Low, Medium, High) based on scenarios and expert consensus. Quick to implement, easy to communicate across non-technical management departments.
Quantitative Analysis Numerical, asset-valued calculations utilizing financial equations ($). Provides precise budget cost-benefit calculations for exact business justifications.

Core Quantitative Calculations

Quantitative calculations allow security professionals to model financial risk using standard formulas:

  • Single Loss Expectancy (SLE): Asset Value × Exposure Factor
  • Annualized Rate of Occurrence (ARO): Number of estimated threat events per year.
  • Annualized Loss Expectancy (ALE): SLE × ARO
