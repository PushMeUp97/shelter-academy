ABOUT THE ACADEMY

Training that fits between shifts and board meetings.

Shelter Academy is a small, focused training library for the people who govern and run shelters — written to be read in one sitting, not enrolled in.

The approach

Four modules, written for the job — not the org chart.

Most governance and risk training is written for large institutions with compliance departments. Shelter Academy starts from the opposite end: a small board, a handful of staff, and not much time. Every module is built around the decisions you actually have to make — what policy to adopt, how to score a risk, what to do in the first ten minutes after an incident — rather than abstract theory.

The content is reference material you can return to, not a course you have to finish in order. Open whichever module matches the problem in front of you today.

What we mean by each word

A quick map of the four modules.

  • Governance Who decides what. Board duties, staff authority, and the core policies a shelter needs in writing before anything else.
  • Risk Assessment Naming what could go wrong before it does, and scoring it honestly enough that the register actually gets used.
  • Risk Response & Reporting What happens after something does go wrong — the first response, who gets told, and how it gets documented.
  • Technology & Security Keeping client records, and sometimes client location, safe from the people who shouldn't have access to them.
What guides the writing

Three things we hold every module to.

Plain language

No policy jargon that needs its own glossary. If a sentence wouldn't make sense read aloud at a board meeting, it gets rewritten.

Built to be used

Every module ends in something concrete — a checklist, a register template, a reporting sequence — not just a summary to file away.

Shelter-specific

Examples and risk categories drawn from how shelters actually operate, not generic corporate risk-management language.
