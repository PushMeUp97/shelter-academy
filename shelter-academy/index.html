Why it exists

Most shelter risk isn't a single bad night — it's a missing policy.

The board that never agreed on a conflict-of-interest policy. The risk register that lives in someone's head instead of a document. The incident that gets handled well in the moment but never gets written down. Shelter Academy exists to close those gaps before they matter, in plain language, in the time you actually have between shifts and board meetings.

Who it's for

Built for the people actually doing the work.

  • Trustees & board members Governance is your job before it's anyone else's. Start with MOD-01 to understand exactly where your responsibility starts and where it hands off to staff.
  • Executive directors & managers You're translating policy into daily practice. MOD-02 and MOD-03 give you a risk register and a reporting workflow you can put in place this month.
  • Frontline staff & volunteers You're the ones who notice problems first. MOD-03's incident reporting steps are written for the person on shift, not the boardroom.
  • Compliance & safeguarding leads Data and physical security intersect constantly. MOD-04 covers both sides of that overlap, including location confidentiality.
